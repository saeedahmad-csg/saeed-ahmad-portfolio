Roamora Travels — AI Travel Advisor

A production RAG-based AI Chatbot integrated into a live travel agency website. Indexes both unstructured policy guidelines (.txt) and structured tour package catalogs (.xlsx). Delivers strict, grounded answers on pricing, hotels, baggage and cancellation — with zero hallucination and transparent source attribution.

  • Multi-source data ingestion (TXT + XLSX)
  • Expandable "Verified Sources" drawer per answer
